Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a prescription?

Medicines marked "Rx Required" need a valid prescription from a registered doctor. After ordering, send a photo of the prescription on WhatsApp so we can verify it before dispatch.

How long does delivery take?

Within Faisalabad we aim for same-day or within 24 hours. Other cities usually take 2–3 business days. Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next day.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 2000. Below that, delivery charges of Rs. 100–200 may apply depending on your area, confirmed on WhatsApp.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, JazzCash and EasyPaisa. For JazzCash / EasyPaisa, send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp to confirm your order.

Can I return a medicine?

Unopened, unused products in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Please read our Return & Refund policy for exceptions.

Need help choosing a medicine?

Chat with our team on WhatsApp — we reply 24/7. Always consult your doctor for medical advice.
